Return and Refund Policy

You can return most items within 7 days of delivery if they are unused and in the original packaging.

Items that are damaged, defective or different from what you ordered can be returned or exchanged free of charge. Send us a photo of the item within 48 hours of delivery.

Refunds: once we receive the returned item, the refund is processed within 5 to 7 working days. Refunds for card payments go back to the same card. For cash on delivery orders we refund to your bank account, Easypaisa or JazzCash wallet.

Non-returnable items: underwear, earrings, perishable food and gift cards cannot be returned.
