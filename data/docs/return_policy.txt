Aap delivery ke 7 din ke andar zyada tar items return kar sakte hain, bashart-e-ke woh istemal na kiye gaye hon aur apni original packaging mein hon.

Agar koi item damaged, defective ya aapke order se mukhtalif ho, to aap use muft return ya exchange karwa sakte hain. Is ke liye delivery ke 48 ghanton ke andar item ki photo humein bhejein.

Refunds: Jab humein returned item mil jayega, to refund 5 se 7 working days ke andar process kar diya jayega. Card se ki gayi payment ka refund usi card mein wapas kiya jayega. Cash on Delivery (COD) orders ka refund aapke bank account, Easypaisa ya JazzCash wallet mein kiya jayega.

Woh items jo return nahi ho sakte: Underwear, earrings, kharab hone wali khanay peenay ki ashya aur gift cards return nahi kiye ja sakte.
