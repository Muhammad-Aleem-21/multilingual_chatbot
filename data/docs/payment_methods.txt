Payment Methods

We accept Cash on Delivery (COD) across Pakistan with an extra COD fee of Rs. 50. We also accept Visa and Mastercard debit and credit cards, Easypaisa, JazzCash and bank transfer.

If your card payment fails, check that online payments are enabled by your bank and that you have enough balance. If money was deducted but no order was created, it is returned to your account automatically within 3 to 5 working days.

Bank transfer: transfer the amount to the DesiMart bank account shown at checkout and send the receipt to our support WhatsApp. Your order is confirmed after we verify the payment.
