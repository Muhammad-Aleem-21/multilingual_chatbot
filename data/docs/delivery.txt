Delivery Information

Delivery time: orders in major cities (Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad and Rawalpindi) arrive in 2 to 3 working days. Other cities take 4 to 6 working days. Remote areas can take up to 8 working days.

Delivery charges: delivery costs Rs. 200 for orders under Rs. 3,000. Orders of Rs. 3,000 or more get free delivery.

Our courier calls you before delivery, so please keep your phone on. If you miss the delivery, the courier tries again on the next working day, up to 3 attempts. After 3 failed attempts the parcel is sent back to us.
